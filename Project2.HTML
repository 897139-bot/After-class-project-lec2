Who is Virat Kohli?

Virat Kohli (born November 5, 1988, in Delhi) is an Indian international cricketer and former all-format captain of the Indian national team. A right-handed top-order batter and occasional right-arm medium-pace bowler, he continues to play One Day Internationals (ODIs) for India and competes in the Indian Premier League (IPL) for Royal Challengers Bengaluru
